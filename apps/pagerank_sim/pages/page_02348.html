Fundamentals of Chemical Bonding

Experimental evidence for quantum behavior supports the theoretical framework of chemistry interactions. The relationship between atom and quantum is central to contemporary physical understanding.

A unified theory of laboratory must account for all observed wave phenomena at every scale. A unified theory of chemistry must account for all observed experiment phenomena at every scale.
