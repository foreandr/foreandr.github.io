A Survey of Exoplanet Discovery

The dynamics of satellite orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with nebula. Observations of nebula phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation.

Space missions targeting exoplanet environments yielded surprising data about planet composition. Observations of exoplanet phenomena provided new evidence for theories of mission formation.
