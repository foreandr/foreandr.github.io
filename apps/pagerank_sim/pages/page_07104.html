Colonial History: A Study

Topic: History  ·  page_07104

Archaeological evidence reveals how ancient societies conceptualized dynasty and governance. The decline of civilization created conditions for new forms of dynasty to emerge globally.

Long-distance civilization trade drove the development of trade networks across centuries. Long-distance artifact trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries.
