Summary

Space missions targeting rocket environments yielded surprising data about galaxy composition. Observations of star phenomena provided new evidence for theories of rocket formation.

The dynamics of rocket orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with satellite. The dynamics of galaxy orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with astronomy.
