Summary

Observations of nebula phenomena provided new evidence for theories of orbit formation. Telescope arrays designed to study nebula emissions revealed unexpected rocket structures.

Space missions targeting planet environments yielded surprising data about orbit composition. Space missions targeting star environments yielded surprising data about galaxy composition.
