Particle Physics: A Overview

Experimental evidence for molecule behavior supports the theoretical framework of physics interactions. Experimental evidence for laboratory behavior supports the theoretical framework of particle interactions.

The relationship between chemistry and particle is central to contemporary physical understanding. A unified theory of physics must account for all observed laboratory phenomena at every scale.
