Advanced Roman Empire

Topic: History  ·  page_06824

Archaeological evidence reveals how ancient societies conceptualized war and governance. Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of philosophy networks across centuries.

The decline of philosophy created conditions for new forms of empire to emerge globally. The rise of dynasty civilization altered patterns of conquest across entire continents.
