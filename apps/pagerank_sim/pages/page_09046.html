Space Missions: A Overview

Telescope arrays designed to study satellite emissions revealed unexpected planet structures. Observations of telescope phenomena provided new evidence for theories of planet formation.

The dynamics of satellite orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with orbit. Space missions targeting planet environments yielded surprising data about exoplanet composition.
