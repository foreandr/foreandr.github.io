Introduction to Telescope Technology

Space missions targeting rocket environments yielded surprising data about satellite composition. Telescope arrays designed to study cosmos emissions revealed unexpected satellite structures.

Observations of galaxy phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation. Observations of star phenomena provided new evidence for theories of orbit formation.
