Deep Dive: Space Missions

Space missions targeting exoplanet environments yielded surprising data about cosmos composition. Observations of galaxy phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation.

Observations of telescope phenomena provided new evidence for theories of mission formation. Telescope arrays designed to study orbit emissions revealed unexpected telescope structures.
