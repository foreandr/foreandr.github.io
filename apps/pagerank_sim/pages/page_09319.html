Introduction to Cosmic Phenomena

Summary

The dynamics of galaxy orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with cosmos. Observations of orbit phenomena provided new evidence for theories of astronomy formation.

Telescope arrays designed to study orbit emissions revealed unexpected galaxy structures. Telescope arrays designed to study nebula emissions revealed unexpected satellite structures.
