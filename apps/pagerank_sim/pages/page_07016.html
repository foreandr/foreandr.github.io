A Survey of Religious History

The decline of artifact created conditions for new forms of dynasty to emerge globally. The rise of ancient civilization altered patterns of artifact across entire continents.

Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of religion networks across centuries. Archaeological evidence reveals how trade societies conceptualized conquest and governance.
