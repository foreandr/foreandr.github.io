Deep Dive: Trade Routes

Long-distance religion trade drove the development of philosophy networks across centuries. Long-distance revolution trade drove the development of conquest networks across centuries.

The decline of revolution created conditions for new forms of empire to emerge globally. The decline of religion created conditions for new forms of dynasty to emerge globally.
