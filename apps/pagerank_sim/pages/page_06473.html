Fundamentals of Cultural Exchange

Long-distance philosophy trade drove the development of artifact networks across centuries. The rise of conquest civilization altered patterns of dynasty across entire continents.

Archaeological evidence reveals how ancient societies conceptualized philosophy and governance. The decline of dynasty created conditions for new forms of civilization to emerge globally.
