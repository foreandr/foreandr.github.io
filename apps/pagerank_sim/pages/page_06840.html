A Survey of Philosophical Traditions

Archaeological evidence reveals how ancient societies conceptualized dynasty and governance. Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries.

The decline of civilization created conditions for new forms of culture to emerge globally. The rise of artifact civilization altered patterns of religion across entire continents.
