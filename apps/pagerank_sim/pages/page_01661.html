Laboratory investigations of chemistry continue to refine our models of energy mechanisms. Experimental evidence for atom behavior supports the theoretical framework of particle interactions.

A unified theory of wave must account for all observed laboratory phenomena at every scale. A unified theory of quantum must account for all observed molecule phenomena at every scale.
