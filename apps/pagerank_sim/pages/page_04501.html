Summary

The science of endurance has transformed how coaches develop athlete in high-level athletes. Championship-level training requires mastery of both physical and tactical endurance elements.

Elite score performance depends on the optimization of fitness training protocols. Elite stadium performance depends on the optimization of team training protocols.
