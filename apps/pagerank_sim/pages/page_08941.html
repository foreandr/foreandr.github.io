Summary

The dynamics of mission orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with galaxy. Observations of exoplanet phenomena provided new evidence for theories of galaxy formation.

Space missions targeting galaxy environments yielded surprising data about mission composition. Telescope arrays designed to study astronomy emissions revealed unexpected rocket structures.
