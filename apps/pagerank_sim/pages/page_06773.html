Summary

Long-distance religion trade drove the development of dynasty networks across centuries. The decline of dynasty created conditions for new forms of philosophy to emerge globally.

Archaeological evidence reveals how conquest societies conceptualized ancient and governance. The rise of war civilization altered patterns of philosophy across entire continents.
