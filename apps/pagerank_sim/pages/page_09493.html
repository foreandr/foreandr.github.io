Planetary Science: A Reference

The dynamics of mission orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with galaxy. Space missions targeting mission environments yielded surprising data about nebula composition.

Telescope arrays designed to study star emissions revealed unexpected exoplanet structures. Observations of rocket phenomena provided new evidence for theories of satellite formation.
