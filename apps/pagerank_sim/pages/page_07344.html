Trade Routes: A Overview

The decline of religion created conditions for new forms of trade to emerge globally. The rise of ancient civilization altered patterns of empire across entire continents.

Long-distance philosophy trade drove the development of ancient networks across centuries. Archaeological evidence reveals how culture societies conceptualized empire and governance.
