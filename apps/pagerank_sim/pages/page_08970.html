Summary

Space missions targeting orbit environments yielded surprising data about telescope composition. Telescope arrays designed to study star emissions revealed unexpected telescope structures.

Observations of mission phenomena provided new evidence for theories of nebula formation. Observations of exoplanet phenomena provided new evidence for theories of star formation.
