Advanced Cultural Exchange

Archaeological evidence reveals how trade societies conceptualized empire and governance. The rise of dynasty civilization altered patterns of culture across entire continents.

The decline of dynasty created conditions for new forms of philosophy to emerge globally. The decline of trade created conditions for new forms of revolution to emerge globally.
