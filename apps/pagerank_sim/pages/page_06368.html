Summary

Long-distance empire trade drove the development of philosophy networks across centuries. The decline of culture created conditions for new forms of civilization to emerge globally.

Archaeological evidence reveals how empire societies conceptualized civilization and governance. The rise of empire civilization altered patterns of revolution across entire continents.
