Modern Particle Physics

Laboratory investigations of physics continue to refine our models of particle mechanisms. Experimental evidence for particle behavior supports the theoretical framework of laboratory interactions.

Experimental evidence for wave behavior supports the theoretical framework of quantum interactions. A unified theory of chemistry must account for all observed laboratory phenomena at every scale.
