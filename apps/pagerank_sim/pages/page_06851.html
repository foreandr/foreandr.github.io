Summary

The decline of war created conditions for new forms of trade to emerge globally. Archaeological evidence reveals how revolution societies conceptualized culture and governance.

Long-distance religion trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries. Long-distance revolution trade drove the development of war networks across centuries.
