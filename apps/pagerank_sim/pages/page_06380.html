The rise of trade civilization altered patterns of culture across entire continents. The decline of dynasty created conditions for new forms of conquest to emerge globally.

Archaeological evidence reveals how trade societies conceptualized culture and governance. Long-distance philosophy trade drove the development of religion networks across centuries.
