Space missions targeting exoplanet environments yielded surprising data about star composition. The dynamics of satellite orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with nebula.

Observations of exoplanet phenomena provided new evidence for theories of satellite formation. Observations of mission phenomena provided new evidence for theories of planet formation.
