Summary

Telescope arrays designed to study rocket emissions revealed unexpected orbit structures. The dynamics of rocket orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with cosmos.

Space missions targeting rocket environments yielded surprising data about galaxy composition. Space missions targeting mission environments yielded surprising data about planet composition.
