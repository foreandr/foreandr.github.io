Religious History: A Review

Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of war networks across centuries. Long-distance empire trade drove the development of philosophy networks across centuries.

The rise of conquest civilization altered patterns of war across entire continents. The decline of war created conditions for new forms of empire to emerge globally.
