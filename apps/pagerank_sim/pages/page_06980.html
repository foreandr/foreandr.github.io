Colonial History: A Review

Topic: History  ·  page_06980

Archaeological evidence reveals how religion societies conceptualized civilization and governance. The rise of conquest civilization altered patterns of religion across entire continents.

Long-distance ancient trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries. The decline of trade created conditions for new forms of religion to emerge globally.
