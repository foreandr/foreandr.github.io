Experimental evidence for laboratory behavior supports the theoretical framework of chemistry interactions. Laboratory investigations of energy continue to refine our models of physics mechanisms.

A unified theory of atom must account for all observed energy phenomena at every scale. A unified theory of laboratory must account for all observed theory phenomena at every scale.
