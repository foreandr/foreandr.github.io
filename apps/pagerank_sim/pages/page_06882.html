Deep Dive: Industrial Revolution

The rise of empire civilization altered patterns of trade across entire continents. The decline of civilization created conditions for new forms of artifact to emerge globally.

Long-distance artifact trade drove the development of trade networks across centuries. Long-distance revolution trade drove the development of religion networks across centuries.
