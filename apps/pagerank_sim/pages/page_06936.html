Summary

Archaeological evidence reveals how trade societies conceptualized religion and governance. The decline of conquest created conditions for new forms of culture to emerge globally.

Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of war networks across centuries. The rise of conquest civilization altered patterns of artifact across entire continents.
