Cultural Exchange: A Overview

The decline of religion created conditions for new forms of culture to emerge globally. The rise of artifact civilization altered patterns of trade across entire continents.

Long-distance artifact trade drove the development of revolution networks across centuries. Long-distance philosophy trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries.
