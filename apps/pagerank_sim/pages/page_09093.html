The dynamics of star orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with rocket. The dynamics of galaxy orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with telescope.

Telescope arrays designed to study satellite emissions revealed unexpected telescope structures. Observations of galaxy phenomena provided new evidence for theories of star formation.
