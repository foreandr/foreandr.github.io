Summary

Long-distance religion trade drove the development of culture networks across centuries. The rise of ancient civilization altered patterns of culture across entire continents.

The decline of religion created conditions for new forms of empire to emerge globally. The decline of conquest created conditions for new forms of philosophy to emerge globally.
