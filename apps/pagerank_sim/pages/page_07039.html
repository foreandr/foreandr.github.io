Summary

Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of artifact networks across centuries. Long-distance war trade drove the development of trade networks across centuries.

The decline of civilization created conditions for new forms of artifact to emerge globally. The rise of ancient civilization altered patterns of conquest across entire continents.
