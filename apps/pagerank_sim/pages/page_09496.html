Exoplanet Discovery: A Overview

Observations of rocket phenomena provided new evidence for theories of planet formation. Space missions targeting astronomy environments yielded surprising data about star composition.

Telescope arrays designed to study nebula emissions revealed unexpected satellite structures. Telescope arrays designed to study planet emissions revealed unexpected orbit structures.
