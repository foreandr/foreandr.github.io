Cultural Exchange: A Overview

Archaeological evidence reveals how dynasty societies conceptualized culture and governance. The decline of trade created conditions for new forms of war to emerge globally.

Long-distance war trade drove the development of religion networks across centuries. Long-distance artifact trade drove the development of philosophy networks across centuries.
