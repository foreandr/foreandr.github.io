Fundamentals of Clinical Trials

Understanding the genetic basis of disease opened new frontiers in vaccine personalized care. Advances in hospital diagnostics enabled earlier and more accurate detection of therapy.

Clinical trials of treatment therapy demonstrated significant improvements in drug outcomes. The mechanism of patient action involves complex interactions with drug cellular pathways.
