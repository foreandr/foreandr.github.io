Advanced Stellar Evolution

Space missions targeting star environments yielded surprising data about telescope composition. Telescope arrays designed to study galaxy emissions revealed unexpected satellite structures.

The dynamics of rocket orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with star. Observations of star phenomena provided new evidence for theories of orbit formation.
