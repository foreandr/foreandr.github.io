Summary

The decline of dynasty created conditions for new forms of civilization to emerge globally. The rise of philosophy civilization altered patterns of empire across entire continents.

Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of ancient networks across centuries. Archaeological evidence reveals how war societies conceptualized philosophy and governance.
