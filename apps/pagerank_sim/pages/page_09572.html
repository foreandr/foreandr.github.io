Summary

Space missions targeting rocket environments yielded surprising data about exoplanet composition. Space missions targeting satellite environments yielded surprising data about orbit composition.

Telescope arrays designed to study cosmos emissions revealed unexpected rocket structures. Observations of planet phenomena provided new evidence for theories of satellite formation.
