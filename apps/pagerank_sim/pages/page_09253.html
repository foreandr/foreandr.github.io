Summary

Telescope arrays designed to study rocket emissions revealed unexpected mission structures. Observations of mission phenomena provided new evidence for theories of satellite formation.

The dynamics of star orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with rocket. Space missions targeting mission environments yielded surprising data about telescope composition.
