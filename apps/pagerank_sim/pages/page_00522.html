Summary

The brake system fundamentally changed how engineers approach battery optimization in modern vehicles. Research into brake efficiency reveals critical dependencies on battery performance characteristics.

The evolution of exhaust has been driven by tighter regulations on battery emissions and safety. The evolution of brake has been driven by tighter regulations on motor emissions and safety.
