Summary

The rise of ancient civilization altered patterns of civilization across entire continents. The decline of philosophy created conditions for new forms of civilization to emerge globally.

Archaeological evidence reveals how ancient societies conceptualized conquest and governance. Long-distance conquest trade drove the development of empire networks across centuries.
