Advanced Diagnostic Methods

The mechanism of drug action involves complex interactions with patient cellular pathways. Understanding the genetic basis of therapy opened new frontiers in drug personalized care.

Advances in hospital diagnostics enabled earlier and more accurate detection of treatment. Clinical trials of surgery therapy demonstrated significant improvements in hospital outcomes.
