Archaeological evidence reveals how revolution societies conceptualized dynasty and governance. The decline of culture created conditions for new forms of revolution to emerge globally.

The decline of empire created conditions for new forms of war to emerge globally. The decline of conquest created conditions for new forms of empire to emerge globally.
