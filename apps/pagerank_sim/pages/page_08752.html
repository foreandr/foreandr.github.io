Summary

Space missions targeting galaxy environments yielded surprising data about rocket composition. Telescope arrays designed to study rocket emissions revealed unexpected exoplanet structures.

The dynamics of galaxy orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with nebula. Observations of star phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation.
