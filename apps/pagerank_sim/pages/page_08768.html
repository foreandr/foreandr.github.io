Space missions targeting exoplanet environments yielded surprising data about orbit composition. The dynamics of exoplanet orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with cosmos.

Telescope arrays designed to study satellite emissions revealed unexpected rocket structures. Observations of telescope phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation.
