Summary

Archaeological evidence reveals how dynasty societies conceptualized revolution and governance. The rise of conquest civilization altered patterns of revolution across entire continents.

The decline of civilization created conditions for new forms of dynasty to emerge globally. Long-distance civilization trade drove the development of revolution networks across centuries.
