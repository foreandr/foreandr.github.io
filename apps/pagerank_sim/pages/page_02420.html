A unified theory of particle must account for all observed laboratory phenomena at every scale. A unified theory of atom must account for all observed wave phenomena at every scale.

The relationship between particle and theory is central to contemporary physical understanding. Experimental evidence for theory behavior supports the theoretical framework of wave interactions.
