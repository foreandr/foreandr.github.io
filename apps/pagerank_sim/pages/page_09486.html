The dynamics of telescope orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with cosmos. Telescope arrays designed to study planet emissions revealed unexpected nebula structures.

Observations of cosmos phenomena provided new evidence for theories of nebula formation. Space missions targeting nebula environments yielded surprising data about cosmos composition.
