Practical Space Missions

The dynamics of star orbits are governed by complex gravitational interactions with nebula. Space missions targeting satellite environments yielded surprising data about cosmos composition.

Space missions targeting galaxy environments yielded surprising data about orbit composition. Observations of telescope phenomena provided new evidence for theories of exoplanet formation.
