What you’re seeing

Field is the sum of retarded 2-D waves (1/√r) from every transmitting antenna. Scope samples that sum at the selected point.

Decomposition is not a literal FFT button—it’s the same math: multiply the total by each local oscillator sin(2πfᵢ(t − rᵢ/c)) and integrate per bit. Bars show how each integrator is building the current symbol; the text is the running bit decision vs each source.
